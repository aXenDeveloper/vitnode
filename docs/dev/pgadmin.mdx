If you generated `postgres` using `docker-compose-dev.yml` you can get access to `pgadmin` on [http://localhost:5050](http://localhost:5050).

## Login

As default, you can login with:

- **Email**: `admin@admin.com`
- **Password**: `admin`

## Connection

To connect to your database you need to create a new connection. To do this, click on `Add New Server` and fill the form with your database credentials.

As default:

- **Host name/address**: `database`
- **Port**: `5432`
- **Maintenance database**: `vitnode`
- **Username**: `root`
- **Password**: `root`
