## Usage

```ts
import {
  Drawer,
  DrawerClose,
  DrawerContent,
  DrawerDescription,
  DrawerFooter,
  DrawerHeader,
  DrawerTitle,
  DrawerTrigger,
} from '@vitnode/core/components/ui/drawer'
```

```tsx
<Drawer>
  <DrawerTrigger asChild>
    <Button variant="outline">Report post</Button>
  </DrawerTrigger>
  <DrawerContent>
    <DrawerHeader>
      <DrawerTitle>What's wrong with this post?</DrawerTitle>
      <DrawerDescription>Moderators see your report.</DrawerDescription>
    </DrawerHeader>
    <DrawerFooter>
      <DrawerClose asChild>
        <Button variant="ghost">Cancel</Button>
      </DrawerClose>
    </DrawerFooter>
  </DrawerContent>
</Drawer>
```

`DrawerTrigger` and `DrawerClose` take `asChild` or Base UI's `render` to use
your own button. Wrap wide content in `mx-auto max-w-sm` so it doesn't stretch
across a desktop.

## Direction and swipe

<Preview name="drawer-direction" />

`direction` picks the edge: `bottom` (default), `top`, `left` or `right`. Swipe
toward that edge to close. A quick flick closes it even from a short drag, a
slow nudge springs back, and the backdrop fades as you drag.

```tsx
<Drawer direction="right">...</Drawer>
```

Bottom drawers get a grab handle and stop at 80% of the screen height. With
reduced motion on, the drawer opens and closes instantly, but dragging still
follows your finger.
