The Brand section collects what makes VitNode look like VitNode, so your
community, plugin or blog post can use it without guessing. Everything here
ships in `@vitnode/core` or downloads straight from the page.

<Preview name="pet" />

## What's here

- **Tabby, the pet.** A striped hexagon cat with 14 states, from `hello` to
  `dizzy`. Its body is the logo's hexagon and its colors are the logo's blues,
  so it never looks off-brand next to the mark.
- **The palette.** Tabby and the logo use the same blues as the UI's primary
  color, documented with every other token on the Colors page.
