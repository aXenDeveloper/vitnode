VitNode keeps your **Terms of Service** and **Privacy Policy** as numbered, frozen versions. Every published version stays readable at a stable address, members get a comparison of what changed, Terms acceptance is recorded per member and version, and every new version is announced by email to every member.

## How the two documents differ

| | Terms of Service | Privacy Policy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Public page | `/terms` | `/privacy` |
| On sign-up | Linked, with an unticked box members must tick | Linked under the form |
| First-time SSO members | Accept on the acceptance page after signing in | Linked before the account is created |
| A new version | Email to everyone. You choose: informational, or fresh acceptance once it takes effect | Email to everyone and an informational notice |
| Member record | Acceptance of the exact version | Only that the notice was dismissed |

The first Terms version always requires acceptance. Nothing changes for an install that has never published a legal document: sign-up, sign-in and every route behave as before until a Terms version takes effect.

Cookie choices and marketing consent stay in their own flows. Accepting the Terms, dismissing a notice and opting into a newsletter are separate records.
