Every published Terms of Service or Privacy Policy version is emailed to every member who had an account when it was announced. The email is a service message: newsletter and notification preferences never suppress it. It carries the document name, version, effective date, your summary of changes, the full-text link, the comparison link and the action needed, if any.

## Follow the delivery

Go to **AdminCP → System → Legal documents → Manage → History** and select **View** on a version.

| Count | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Recipients | Members on the list, one row per member and version |
| Sent to provider | Your email provider accepted the message. It does not prove it reached the inbox |
| Waiting | Not sent yet, or scheduled for another attempt |
| Failed | Every attempt failed (five per member) |
| Skipped | Not sent: no usable address, an unverified address, or an account being deleted. Listed apart from failures |

The recipients table (with the **View member delivery and acceptance records** permission) shows each member's status, attempts, last error and the provider's message id. Opening the email or following a link never counts as accepting the Terms.

## How sending works

1. Publishing commits the version and a fan-out job together.
2. The fan-out adds members in batches of 500 and resumes from where it stopped. Running it again never adds a member twice.
3. Emails go out in batches of 25 with a per-member idempotency key, so a provider that supports it (for example Resend) drops a duplicate. Each address is checked again right before sending.
4. A failed send is retried with backoff. A `legal-delivery` cron job every ten minutes restarts any announcement whose queue tasks stopped.

The queue and cron must run, as for all [cron jobs](/docs/dev/cron).

## Recover from an outage

When every send in a batch fails, the card shows **The email provider keeps failing** with the time and the last error. Sending pauses for five minutes, then tries again on its own; a successful send clears the warning.

<Steps>

<Step>

### Fix the provider

Check the email adapter's credentials and the provider's status, then send a test from **AdminCP → System → Integrations → Email → Test email**.

</Step>

<Step>

### Retry the failed emails

Select **Retry failed emails** on the version, or **Retry** on one row. Failed deliveries get a fresh set of attempts and keep their idempotency key, then send right away.

</Step>

</Steps>

## Check the result

The **Failed** count drops to zero, **Sent to provider** grows and the outage warning is gone. **AdminCP → Staff → Administrators → Logs** records each retry.
