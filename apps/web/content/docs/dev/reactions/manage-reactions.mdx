The reactions members can use, what they are worth and who may use them are AdminCP settings. This page adds a reaction and limits it for one role.

## Before you begin

Sign in to the AdminCP with an admin who holds `reactions:can_manage` (a root administrator holds it) and, for role limits, `roles:can_edit`.

<Steps>

<Step>

### Open the reactions

Open **AdminCP → Users → Reactions**. A new installation starts with seven reactions, from **Like** (+1) to **Disagree** (-1). Drag the handles to change the order members see.

<ImgDocs
  src={list}
  alt="AdminCP Reactions page listing Like, Love, Haha, Wow, Sad, Thanks and Disagree with their points and an Enabled switch"
/>

</Step>

<Step>

### Add a reaction

Click **Add reaction**. The dialog has four steps: **Look**, **Details**, **Reputation** and **Permissions**. Click **Next**, or jump to a step in the list. A tick means the step is filled in, and red means it needs a fix. The preview on the left shows the reaction the way members will see it.

Start with its look:

- **Emoji or icon**: any emoji, or a Lucide icon drawn in your theme's text color. One picker has both, in two tabs.
- **Upload**: drop or choose a PNG, JPEG, WebP or GIF up to 512 KB. Animated GIFs work, so the party parrot is allowed. People who prefer reduced motion see a still first frame. Big images are scaled to fit 256x256.

Then fill in the **Title** members see, a **Code** such as `party-parrot`, and the **Reputation points**, from -100 to 100.

<ImgDocs
  src={dialog}
  alt="The Edit reaction dialog with a live preview and the Look, Details, Reputation and Permissions steps"
/>

Under **Permissions**, leave **All roles can use it** on, or turn it off and check the roles that may. Either way, give a role a daily limit or leave it **Unlimited**. The **Add reaction** button is on the last step.

Changing the points of an existing reaction applies to past reactions too. VitNode recounts the reputation of everyone who received it in the background. **Recount reputation** recounts everyone. It asks first, because it replaces each member's reputation with their reaction total, so reputation from anywhere else, like a manual edit, is lost.

</Step>

<Step>

### Set role limits

Open **AdminCP → Users → Roles**, edit a role and open the **Reactions** tab:

<ImgDocs
  src={roleTab}
  alt="The Reactions tab of the Edit role dialog with Can react, Can react to own content, Reactions per item and Reactions per day"
/>

| Setting                        | Default   | What it does                                                                                                                  |
| ------------------------------ | --------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Can react**                  | On        | Members with this role can react. Off for the Guest role.                                                                     |
| **Can react to own content**   | On        | Members can react to posts and comments they wrote. Those reactions never earn them reputation.                               |
| **Reactions per item**         | 1         | Different reactions one member gives one item. At 1, a new pick replaces the old one; above 1, the picker stops at the limit. |
| **Reactions per day**          | Unlimited | Every reaction counts. The count resets at midnight in the member's time zone.                                                |

A member with several roles gets the most generous value of each setting. Click **Save changes**.

Which reactions a role may use, and how many of each a day, live on the reaction itself: edit it and open the **Permissions** step.

</Step>

</Steps>

## Check the result

Sign in as a member of the role and open an article with reactions. The picker shows only the reactions the role may use, a limited one shows how many are left today, and a used-up one is disabled until midnight.
