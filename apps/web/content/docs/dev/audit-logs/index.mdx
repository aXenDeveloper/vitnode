Activity Logs (audit logs) are where VitNode answers "wait, who changed
this?". They record administrative, moderation, plugin and content operations
in one place. Every entry says who acted, from
which area (AdminCP, ModeratorCP, the site or the system), which plugin and
action it was, which resource it touched and what changed. You read them in the
AdminCP, next to your staff:

- **Admin logs**: **AdminCP → Staff → Administrators → Logs**
  (`/admin/core/staff/admins/logs`). AdminCP changes, plus what members,
  plugins and scheduled jobs did.
- **Moderator logs**: **AdminCP → Staff → Moderators → Logs**
  (`/admin/core/staff/moderators/logs`). What moderators did: reports,
  approvals and hidden content.

Each staff list has a **Logs** button that opens its log.

Search the target, action and actor name from the box above the table. Actions
match by the label you see, in your language, with or without accents - so
"zawieszono" finds "Zawieszono konto". To narrow
the list, open **Filters**: a panel with a **Plugin**, **Action** and **Actor**
select (plus **Area** in the admin log). Pick what you need and press
**Show results**. **Date** sits beside it and offers today, the last 7 or 30
days, or your own from-to range picked in a calendar. On a phone, the filters
and dates open in a drawer from the **Filters** button instead. The admin log
shows an **Area** column only when the entries on screen come from more than
one area. **Export CSV** sits next to the page title. The log is paged: pick a page or a page size under the table, and the URL
keeps the filters, the page and the page size.

Click an entry to open its details right under its row, one entry at a time:
each changed field before and after, anything else the operation recorded (such
as a warning's points and expiry), an **Open** link to the target, **Show this
person's activity**, and the date, plugin, IP address and request id. Click it
again, or press Escape, to close it. The open entry is in the URL too, so you
can share a link to it.

## What gets recorded without any code

| Area            | Recorded automatically                                                                                                                  |
| --------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Content Engine  | Create, edit, delete, duplicate, publish, unpublish, hide, unhide, restore, schedule and every translation change of every content type; access rule changes and protected files |
| Roles and staff | Roles created, edited and deleted; administrator and moderator permissions added, changed and removed                                   |
| Users           | Profile and role changes made in the AdminCP, password resets, email verification, signed-out devices                                   |
| Settings        | AI settings and access, notification types and danger-zone actions, report reasons, warning reasons and thresholds, and log exports |
| Moderation      | Taking, assigning, resolving and reopening reports, report notes, acting on reported content, approving and rejecting submissions, [warnings and suspensions](/docs/dev/moderation/warnings) |
| Background work | Scheduled publishing, cron jobs and queue tasks, attributed to the system; manual cron runs, attributed to the admin who started them   |
| Payments        | Checkouts started by members, and every status change the provider reports (processing, succeeded, failed, expired)                     |

Your plugin's own operations, such as creating a note or refunding an order,
take one call. See [Record plugin events](/docs/dev/audit-logs/record-plugin-events).

## Audit logs, revisions and technical logs

VitNode keeps three kinds of history, and each one answers a different
question.

| History                                             | Answers                                           | Stored in                |
| --------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Activity Logs                                       | Who did what, when, to which resource             | `core_audit_logs`        |
| [Content revisions](/docs/content-engine/editorial) | What a record's content used to be, restorable    | `core_content_revisions` |
| [Technical logs](/docs/dev/debugging/logging)       | Warnings and errors for whoever debugs the server | `core_logs`              |

For an editorial content type, the audit entry points at the revision it
created instead of copying the content. The entry's details then show what
changed since the previous revision.
