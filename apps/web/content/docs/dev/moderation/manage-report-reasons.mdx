Report reasons are the choices a member sees first in the report dialog, like **Spam** or **Abusive content**. You manage them in the AdminCP, with a title and a description in every language your site serves, so the list fits your community instead of a fixed one.

A new installation starts with four reasons: **Spam**, **Abusive content**, **Rule violation** and **Other**. **Other** asks the member to explain. Sites upgraded from the fixed list get the same four on their next `pnpm db:migrate`, and existing reports keep their reason.

## Before you begin

You need the **Report reasons** admin permissions, or a root role. Each permission covers one action: **View report reasons**, **Create report reasons**, **Edit and reorder report reasons** and **Delete report reasons**. Grant them in **AdminCP → Staff → Administrators**.

<Steps>

<Step>

### Open the list

Open **AdminCP → Moderation → Report reasons**. The list is in the order members see it, and the **Details** column shows which reasons make the member explain.

  src={reasonsList}
  alt="Report reasons page in the AdminCP: Spam, Abusive content, Rule violation and Other with their descriptions, a drag handle on each row, Optional or Required in the Details column, edit and delete buttons, and an Add reason button"
/>

</Step>

<Step>

### Add a reason

Choose **Add reason**. Write a short **Title** and a one-line **Description**, then pick another language from the menu next to each field to translate it. A language you leave empty falls back to the site's default language.

Turn on **Require details** when moderators cannot act on the reason without an explanation, as with **Other**. The member then has to write at least 10 characters before the report is sent.

<ImgDocs
  src={reasonSheet}
  alt="Add report reason panel with an English Title field set to Off-topic, a Description field, a language menu next to each field, a Require details switch and Cancel and Add reason buttons"
/>

Choose **Add reason**. The reason goes to the end of the list, and members can pick it right away.

</Step>

<Step>

### Put the most common reason first

Drag a row by its handle to move it. With a keyboard, focus the handle, press Space, move with the arrow keys and press Space again to drop. The order saves on drop.

</Step>

</Steps>

## Edit or delete a reason

Choose the pencil to edit a reason. Reports already filed with it show the new title, because a report stores which reason was picked, not its text.

Choose the trash can to delete a reason. Members can no longer pick it, and reports filed with it stay, labeled **Removed reason** in the [Moderator CP](/docs/dev/moderation/process-reports).

## Limits

- At most 10 reasons. **Add reason** is disabled at 10 and the API refuses an 11th with `409 limit_reached`.
- At least one reason. The last one cannot be deleted (`409 last_reason`), so the dialog always has something to offer.
- Titles are up to 100 characters, descriptions up to 255.

## Check the result

Open a published article as a signed-in member and choose **Report**. The dialog lists your reasons in your order, in the member's language, and a reason with **Require details** asks **What should moderators know?** before **Send report**.

The routes and events behind this page are in the [moderation reference](/docs/dev/moderation/reference#report-reasons).
