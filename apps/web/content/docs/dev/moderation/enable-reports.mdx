---
title: Enable reports
description: Where to turn on reporting for VitNode content and for your own plugin's items, and what the moderators need next.
icon: Flag
---

Readers report content with a **Report** button, and every report lands in the moderators' queue. How you turn it on depends on what is being reported:

| What readers report                         | Guide                                                                                   |
| ------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Records of a Content Engine content type    | [Report content](/docs/content-engine/reports): the `reports` option and `ReportButton` |
| Anything else your plugin stores            | [Custom report targets](/docs/dev/moderation/reference#custom-report-targets) with `defineReportTarget` |

Then set up the people who handle the reports:

1. [Manage report reasons](/docs/dev/moderation/manage-report-reasons) to choose what readers can pick.
2. [Configure moderators](/docs/dev/moderation/configure-moderators) to give someone `can_view_reports` and `can_manage_reports`.
3. [Process reports](/docs/dev/moderation/process-reports) to work the queue in the ModCP.
