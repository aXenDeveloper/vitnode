The **Moderator CP** at `/modcp/reports` is where moderators work through reports, signed in with their normal account. This guide takes a spam report about a blog article from new to closed.

## Before you begin

You need a moderator entry with `can_view_reports` for the content type, and `can_manage_reports` to change anything. Hiding needs `can_hide`. See [Configure moderators](/docs/dev/moderation/configure-moderators).

<Steps>

<Step>

### Open the board

Choose **Moderator CP** in the user menu. **Reports** is a board with three columns, **New**, **In progress** and **Done**. It holds the reports you may view, newest first in each column:

  withoutBackground
  className="*:max-w-full"
  src={queue}
  alt="Moderator CP with the side menu on the left and the reports board on the right: a search box and an Assigned filter above three columns, New, In progress and Done, each holding report cards with the reason, the title, the report number, the content type, the assignee and when it was reported"
/>

Search by title or report number above the board. Type at least three characters, like `#12` or `gift`. **Assigned** narrows the board to your reports or the unassigned ones.

Search and the filter live in the address, so you can send a filtered board to another moderator as a link. Each column loads 20 cards at a time, and **Show more** loads the next 20.

The pill next to the reason, like **+1**, means other people reported the same content. It only counts reports you may view. Reports outside your scopes never appear, not even in the column counts.

Drag a card to another column to move it, and the column tells you what will happen before you let go:

| Move | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **New** → **In progress** | The report is assigned to you. |
| **In progress** → **New** | The assignee is removed. |
| Any column → **Done** | The close dialog opens, so you pick an outcome first. |
| **Done** → **New** or **In progress** | The reopen dialog asks for a reason. |

The card moves right away. If another moderator changed the report first, you get a message and the card slides back to where the server says it belongs. Cards always sit in date order, so a moved card lands where its date puts it, not where you dropped it.

On a touch screen, press and hold a card to pick it up. With a keyboard, focus the grip next to a card's **…** menu, press `Space`, use the arrow keys to pick a column and press `Space` again. The **…** menu has the same moves under **Move to**.

</Step>

<Step>

### Read the report

Open a report. The heading names the content type and its author. The main card starts with the **Description**, the reporter's own words, followed by the **Snapshot**, which is what the reporter saw at the moment they filed it. Choose **View full snapshot** to read it in a side panel. It never changes, even if the article was edited, its revisions were pruned or it was deleted.

On the right, **Details** opens with the reported content: its type, its title linked to the live page and the plugin it belongs to. Below are the assignee, the reporter and the author with their role colors, the reason and the **Visibility** of the content right now. **Linked reports** lists other reports about the same content, each with its reporter and the start of their text.

The snapshot names the language it was captured in. When the reader asked for another language and was served a fallback, it shows both. Files appear as names and sizes only. The report never copies the file itself.

</Step>

<Step>

### Take the report

Drag the card from **New** to **In progress**, or open the report and choose **Start progress** in the status button (**New**), or **Assign to me** under the assignee. The report is assigned to you and moves to **In progress**. To hand it over, open the assignee and pick another moderator; the list shows only moderators who may manage this report. **Stop progress** unassigns you and moves it back to **New**.

When two moderators take the same report at once, one wins and the other sees **Another moderator took this report first**. Every change carries the version you loaded, so acting on a stale page refreshes it instead of overwriting someone else's work.

</Step>

<Step>

### Note and act

Under **Activity**, choose **Add an internal note** or press `M`. Notes use the rich-text editor, and `Cmd+Enter` (`Ctrl+Enter` on Windows) saves them. They are visible to moderators only, never to the reporter or the author. **Notes** is the tab that opens first; **All** and **History** show the rest of the activity.

To change a note, open its **…** menu and choose **Edit** or **Delete**. You can always edit and delete your own notes. Editing or deleting another moderator's notes needs `can_edit_notes` or `can_delete_notes`. An edited note shows **(edited)**, and **History** records every edit with the text before and after, and every deletion with the removed text.

Open **Actions** next to the status button, choose **Hide content** and confirm. The article leaves the public site, search and the sitemap at once, exactly like [hiding it from the AdminCP](/docs/content-engine/content-visibility). It gets a revision, the `hidden` event fires and VitNode revalidates the caches. The activity records the action only when it succeeds. **Restore content** brings it back.

</Step>

<Step>

### Close the report

Drag the card to **Done**, or open the status button on the report and choose **Close report**. Pick an outcome, **No violation**, **Action taken** or **Duplicate**, and add an explanation for the other moderators if you like. When you already hid the content, **Action taken** is picked for you. **Close report** moves it to **Done**. Other reports about the same content stay open, because each reporter's report closes on its own.

On a closed report, the status button offers **Reopen**, which needs a reason that goes into the activity. Dragging the card out of **Done** asks for the same reason.

</Step>

</Steps>

## Check the result

The report sits in the **Done** column, and its activity lists the report, the assignment, your note, the hide and the resolution with names and times. Open the article signed out: it answers `404` while hidden. Open it as the moderator: it loads with the hidden notice, because `can_view_hidden` lets moderators inspect hidden content without any AdminCP edit permission.

## Who sees what

- **Reporters** see only their own toast. Nothing about the report is public.
- **Moderators** with `can_view_reports` see the report, the reporter's name, the snapshot and the activity, for content in their scopes. Without it, a direct URL answers like a report that does not exist.
- **Moderators** with `can_manage_reports` can also take, reassign, note, resolve and reopen, and edit or delete their own notes.
- **Moderators** with `can_edit_notes` or `can_delete_notes` can edit or delete any moderator's notes.
- **Authors** of the reported content never learn who reported it.

When content moves to a resource outside a moderator's scope, the report moves with it. The moderator loses access, and if the report was assigned to them, VitNode unassigns it and logs that in the activity. When the content is deleted, the report keeps its last known scope.
