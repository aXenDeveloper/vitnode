VitNode moderation lets signed-in users report content, and lets moderators review those reports and act on the content from the public site. It is built from three parts that core provides and any plugin can use: reports, the **Moderator CP** (ModCP) and scoped moderator permissions.

| Part                 | What it does                                                                                                   |
| -------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Reports              | A report stores the reason, the reporter's details and a server-side snapshot of what the reporter saw          |
| Moderator CP         | `/modcp/reports` on the public site: a reports board, the snapshot, assignment, notes, resolution and content actions. `/modcp/approvals` holds content waiting for approval |
| Scoped permissions   | A moderator permission can cover every resource of a family, such as every forum section, or only some of them |

A moderator signs in with their normal user session. The ModCP never asks for an AdminCP session, and the server authorizes every ModCP request.

## How the parts fit together

1. A content type opts in with `reports: true`. This registers a **report target** and adds report permissions to the moderator catalog. Nobody holds them until an administrator grants them.
2. A reader clicks **Report** on the article page. The browser sends only the article id, the language, the reason and the details. The server checks that the reader can read the article, then stores a snapshot of it.
3. A moderator opens the ModCP. The reports board shows only reports the moderator may view, filtered before anything is counted.
4. The moderator takes the report, writes internal notes, hides the article if needed and closes the report with an outcome. Every step lands in the report's timeline.

Reports belong to core, not to the plugin. They keep their snapshot after the article is edited, after its revisions are pruned, after it is deleted and even after its plugin is uninstalled.

## What is not included yet

This version covers reports, hiding and restoring content, and [reviewing approvals](/docs/dev/moderation/review-approvals). It has no warnings, bans, automatic moderation, anonymous reports, report notifications or permanent deletion from the ModCP. Moderators still have to read things themselves, sorry.
