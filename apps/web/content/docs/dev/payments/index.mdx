VitNode Payments lets any plugin sell something: a one-time purchase or a
fixed-price monthly or yearly subscription. Core takes the money and keeps the
books. Your plugin decides what the purchase unlocks.

## Who owns what

| Part         | Owns                                                                                                                                       |
| ------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Core**     | Checkout, purchase and subscription records, the webhook inbox, the queue jobs, reconciliation, the billing settings screen and the AdminCP |
| **Provider** | Talking to the payment service: hosted checkout, signatures, customer portal. `@vitnode/stripe` is the first one                           |
| **Plugin**   | Its offers, prices, who may buy, and what a payment unlocks - written in its own tables                                                    |

A plugin never talks to Stripe and never sees a Stripe object. It declares an
offer, and core calls the offer's handlers with plain snapshots when something
happened.

## How a purchase flows

1. The signed-in buyer picks an offer, a currency and (for a plan) an interval.
2. Core resolves the price on the server, checks eligibility and stores the purchase.
3. Core creates a hosted checkout and sends the buyer to it.
4. The provider calls the webhook. Core verifies it, stores it and queues the work.
5. The queue re-reads the payment from the provider and marks the purchase paid.
6. Your offer's handler runs once, in a transaction, and grants access.

The buyer's return from the checkout page proves nothing on its own. The page
asks the server, and the server asks the provider. See
[Reliability](/docs/dev/payments/reliability).

## What is included

- One-time purchases and fixed-price monthly/yearly subscriptions
- Several currencies with explicit prices, formatted with `Intl.NumberFormat`
- Stripe-hosted Checkout and the Stripe Customer Portal
- Refund and dispute tracking for payments made in Stripe
- Billing history in **Settings → Billing** and an AdminCP **Payments** section

Not included: coupons, sales, trials, wallets or credits, carts, guest checkout,
metered billing, plan changes or proration, paid roles, refunds from the
AdminCP, and manual or offline payments.

<Callout type="warn" title="Not a tax or invoicing system">
  Payments collects money. It does not calculate tax and does not issue legal
  invoices. Receipts come from the provider. Check what your country requires
  before selling.
</Callout>
